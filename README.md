### Hi, I'm Bryan

I'm a Computer Scientist and software engineer who builds tools to get rid of tedious work: scripts,
CLIs and small services that take something messy and make it just work. If I
have to do something by hand twice, I usually end up writing a tool for it.

I care a lot about **owning my own data**. Most of my side projects start
with a service that holds my photos, contacts or messages in a format nobody
can use, and end with a tool that gets them back out properly.

Away from the keyboard I love to travel, which is part of why I prefer
working remotely. Give me a good connection and a clear problem and I'll do
my best work from anywhere.

#### What I'd like software to be

I'd like technology to work for people, not for the ecosystem they bought into:

- **Sharing doesn't depend on your phone.** Sending photos, videos or files to someone should just work, whatever phone or platform either of us uses.
- **Music doesn't need a matching subscription.** I should be able to share a song or a playlist with a friend even if we pay for different services, or neither of us does.
- **Movie night doesn't need a pile of subscriptions.** Getting online and watching a film together shouldn't start with working out whether it's on Disney+, Netflix or Prime, then paying for all three to watch the same kind of thing.
- **Leaving is as easy as joining.** Your data should come out of big tech in formats you can actually use, so you can move on without losing access to good technology.

#### What I build

- 📸 **[snapchat-export-tools](https://github.com/talk2bryan/snapchat-export-tools)**: turns a Snapchat "My Data" export into correctly dated, geotagged photos and videos, with captions burned in, multi-snaps joined back together and chat media sorted by conversation. Ready for Immich, Apple Photos or Google Photos.
- 📇 **[contactlookup](https://github.com/talk2bryan/contactlookup)**: a self-hosted API for searching contacts in a VCF file. On [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/contactlookup/).
- 🧪 **[vcf-generator](https://github.com/talk2bryan/vcf-generator)**: generates VCF files full of realistic fake contacts for testing. On [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/vcf-generator/).
- 🗂️ **[trello_card_cli](https://github.com/talk2bryan/trello_card_cli)**: adds Trello cards from the terminal.

#### Homelab

I run my own homelab, built to fix itself and to recover remotely. Immich,
Jellyfin, Nextcloud and Navidrome instead of the cloud versions. AdGuard for
DNS, Tailscale and Cloudflare Tunnel for access, passkeys for sign-in,
Uptime Kuma and ntfy for alerts, Ollama for local LLMs, and tested backups
spread across several locations. I write everything down, so any of it can
be rebuilt from the docs.

#### How I work

- **Make it boring to run.** Tests, CI, versioned releases, multi-arch Docker images and a README that actually tells you how to use it.
- **Solve the real problem.** I'd rather read the raw data and find what ties it together (a timestamp, an ID) than guess.
- **Leave it better documented than I found it.** Runbooks, design notes and diagrams, for teammates and for future me.
